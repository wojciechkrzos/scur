# Free Art Asset Candidates

Researched on 2026-06-17 for possible replacements or paint-over bases for enemies, player, and blockers.

## Strongest Fits

- OpenGameArt - Bullet Hell Sprites
  - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/bullet-hell-sprites
  - License: CC0
  - Best use: small bullet hell player/enemy sprite base; easy to recolor into red, black, and white.

- Kenney - Space Shooter Extension
  - URL: https://kenney.nl/assets/space-shooter-extension
  - License: Creative Commons CC0
  - Best use: modular player/enemy ships or hostile icons; clean shapes that can be palette-swapped.

- OpenGameArt - Space Shooter Redux
  - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/space-shooter-redux
  - License: public-domain/CC0 style pack by Kenney
  - Best use: player, enemies, pickups, and UI placeholders; broad pack, but sci-fi leaning.

- OpenGameArt - Bullet Collection 1
  - URL: https://opengameart.org/content/bullet-collection-1-m484
  - License: public domain
  - Best use: replacing or augmenting projectile/blocker visuals; attribution not required.

## Worth Reviewing

- itch.io - Free Bullet Hell assets
  - URL: https://itch.io/game-assets/free/tag-bullet-hell
  - Best use: discovery page for free packs. Check each pack license individually before importing.

- itch.io - Free Bullet Hell + Top-Down assets
  - URL: https://itch.io/game-assets/free/tag-bullet-hell/tag-top-down
  - Best use: top-down enemies and props that may fit bullet heaven blockers better than spaceship packs.

## Recommendation

Start with Kenney Space Shooter Extension for player/enemy silhouettes and OpenGameArt Bullet Collection 1 for projectiles or blockers. Both have permissive licenses and simple forms that should survive a strict red/black/white palette pass.
